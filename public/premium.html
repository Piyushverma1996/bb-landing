Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does bridal makeup cost in West Delhi?

HD bridal ₹18,000–22,000, airbrush ₹25,000–30,000, engagement/Roka/Sagan ₹10,000–15,000, and reception ₹12,000–16,000. Every quote includes hairstyling, draping, lashes and a touch-up kit - no hidden charges and no GST.

Should I choose HD or airbrush bridal makeup?

Airbrush for long summer wedding days and maximum staying power (10–14 hours); HD for a soft, dewy finish or winter weddings. At your trial, Urvashi tests both on your skin in daylight and recommends honestly.

Do you travel for the wedding, or is it at the studio?

Bridal and party makeup are done on location - Urvashi travels across West Delhi and Delhi NCR to your home or venue. Nail extensions, beauty services and trials happen at the Ramesh Nagar studio.

Where is the Blushes & Brushes studio?

B 1/1 Double Storey, opposite Subway, Ramesh Nagar, New Delhi 110015 - a two-minute walk from Ramesh Nagar metro station (Blue Line).

How much are nail extensions?

Gel nail extensions start at ₹499 with complimentary nail art. Luxe French from ₹1,200 and designer sets (ombré, chrome) from ₹1,500, at the Ramesh Nagar studio.

Do you offer a trial before the wedding?

Yes - bridal trials are ₹3,000 at the studio and are adjusted against your final booking. We recommend the trial 4–8 weeks before the wedding.

How early should I book my date?

For Delhi's Nov–Feb peak wedding season, book 3–4 months ahead - dates fill fast. A small refundable advance holds your slot.
